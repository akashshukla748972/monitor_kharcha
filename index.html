Privacy Policy

Monitor Kharcha • Effective Date: September 2026

🛡️ Summary: Monitor Kharcha is designed as an offline-first, privacy-focused expense tracking tool. Your financial data, budgets, receipts, and PINs remain stored locally on your device. We do not sell, rent, or share your data with any third parties or advertising networks.

1. Information We Collect

Monitor Kharcha is designed from the ground up to minimize data collection. The information handled by the app includes:

  • Financial Records: Transaction titles, amounts, categories, dates, payment modes, and notes that you manually enter into the app. This data is stored locally in an encrypted SQLite database on your device.
  • Receipt Images: Photos of bills or receipts that you choose to capture using your camera or upload from your device gallery. These images remain saved locally on your device.
  • Profile & Budget Settings: Your preferred currency, monthly budget limits, and user display name.

2. Data Storage & Offline Security

Monitor Kharcha operates completely offline. All core bookkeeping functionalities work without an active internet connection:

  • Your data never leaves your device unless you explicitly configure the optional cloud backup.
  • We do not employ any third-party tracking, profiling, or behavioral advertising SDKs.
  • You have the ability to export your entire ledger into PDF or Excel CSV formats at any time.

3. Biometric & PIN Security

The application provides optional security features to protect your financial records:

  • Biometric Authentication: Fingerprint and Face Unlock use the official Android BiometricPrompt system framework. Monitor Kharcha never accesses, processes, or stores your biometric data; verification is handled entirely by your operating system.
  • 4-Digit Security PIN: Your optional 4-digit PIN is stored locally on your device and is never transmitted to any external server.

4. Device Permissions

The app may request the following device permissions for specific user-initiated features:

  • Camera (android.permission.CAMERA): Used solely to allow you to photograph physical receipts or paper invoices to attach to a transaction.
  • Storage / Media Images (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES, READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE): Used solely to let you pick receipt photos from your gallery and to save your exported PDF/Excel statements to your device storage.
  • Biometrics (USE_BIOMETRIC): Used solely to authenticate you when unlocking the app.
  • Vibration (VIBRATE): Provides haptic feedback when pressing keys on the numeric keypad.

5. Third-Party Sharing & Advertising

We do NOT sell, trade, license, or transfer your financial or personal data to outside parties, marketing companies, data brokers, or advertising networks. There are zero third-party ads in Monitor Kharcha.

6. Children's Privacy

Monitor Kharcha is a general audience personal finance application and does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

7. Account & Data Deletion Policy

Monitor Kharcha gives you complete sovereignty and control over your personal account and financial data. You may request deletion of your entire account or specific data points at any time.

ℹ️ Account Deletion Notice: Deleting your account will permanently wipe your profile, security PIN, financial ledgers, and all synced records from our Cloud Firestore database and local storage. This action cannot be undone.

How to Delete Your Account and Associated Data

You can delete your account using either of the following methods:

  • Method 1: In-App Deletion (Immediate)
    1. Open Monitor Kharcha on your device.
    2. Navigate to the Profile tab.
    3. Tap on Switch / Manage Accounts.
    4. Select Delete Account and confirm your 4-digit PIN. Your local records and Cloud Firestore sync records will be immediately and permanently removed.
  • Method 2: Web / Email Request (Without Reinstalling)

    If you have uninstalled the app or cannot access your device, send an email to support@monitorkharcha.app with the subject "Account Deletion Request - Monitor Kharcha".

    Please include the phone number or username associated with your account. Our team will verify and permanently purge your account and all associated cloud data within 48 hours.

Types of Data Deleted vs. Kept

  • Data that is permanently deleted: User profile (name, avatar, phone number), security PIN, all transaction entries, income/expense records, categories, budget limits, receipt photo references, and all cloud database sync records.
  • Data that is retained: None. We do not keep any personal or financial records once deletion is processed.
  • Retention Period: Local data is erased instantly upon in-app deletion. Cloud sync backups are permanently expunged within a maximum of 30 days.

Deleting Partial Data Without Deleting Your Account

You can also selectively delete records without deleting your account:

  • Delete individual expense or income transactions from the transaction list.
  • Permanently clear the in-app Recycle Bin.
  • Reset monthly budget limits and categories from Profile settings.
