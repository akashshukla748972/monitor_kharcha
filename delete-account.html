Account & Data Deletion Request

Application: Monitor Kharcha • Developer: Monitor Kharcha Team
🛡️ Privacy Policy

At Monitor Kharcha, we respect your privacy and give you full control over your personal data. In compliance with Google Play's User Data policy, this page outlines the procedures for permanently deleting your account and all associated data.

⚠️ Permanent Action: Account deletion is irreversible. Once processed, your account profile, 4-digit PIN, transaction history, categories, and synchronized cloud backups will be permanently purged.

1. How to Request Account Deletion

Option A: In-App Deletion (Instant & Automated)

If you have access to the Monitor Kharcha app installed on your device:

  1. Open the Monitor Kharcha app.
  2. Tap the Profile icon in the bottom navigation bar.
  3. Tap on Switch / Manage Accounts.
  4. Locate your active profile and tap Delete Account.
  5. Confirm your 4-digit PIN when prompted. Your local records and all Cloud Firestore sync data are deleted immediately.

Option B: Web / Email Request (Without App Installation)

If you have uninstalled the app or lost device access, you can submit a manual deletion request:

  • Send an email to: support@monitorkharcha.app
  • Subject: Account Deletion Request - Monitor Kharcha
  • Content: Specify the registered Phone Number or User ID linked to your account.

Our support team will process and permanently remove your user record and cloud sync storage within 48 hours.

2. Data Types Deleted vs. Retained

  • Data Permanently Deleted:
    • Account credentials (Phone number, User ID, PIN, security questions)
    • User profile details (Display name, custom avatar, local profile photo)
    • All transaction entries (Expense/Income records, dates, payment modes, notes)
    • Budget configurations, custom categories, and recycled bin items
    • All Cloud Firestore synchronized documents under your user node
  • Data Retained: None. We do not retain any user or financial data once an account is deleted.
  • Retention Period: In-app deletions take effect immediately. Email requests are completed within 48 hours, with all backup mirrors fully expunged within 30 days.

3. Requesting Partial Data Deletion

Users who wish to delete financial records without deleting their entire user account can:

  • Swipe or select any transaction in the ledger to move it to the Recycle Bin or delete it permanently.
  • Empty the Recycle Bin from within the app settings at any time.
  • Modify or clear budget amounts and categories in Profile settings.
